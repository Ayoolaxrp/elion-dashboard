# Competition Demo Script

Target length: 2–3 minutes.

This script is based on what is actually live or demonstrable on ELION today. It does not invent customers, revenue, ROI, or testimonials.

## 0:00–0:20 — Opening

Say:

> “Hi, I’m Ayoola, and I built ELION — an AI operations platform designed to help growing businesses find where they’re losing leads, and then automate the workflows responsible for those leaks.”

Then immediately open the product.

What to show:

- the homepage
- the headline: “Find the leaks in your business. Then automate them.”

## 0:20–0:50 — Run an audit and expose a leak

Say something like:

> “Instead of starting by selling a generic AI chatbot, ELION starts by diagnosing the business.”

What to do:

- go to the funnel or audit entry
- submit a real website
- wait for the audit result

When the result appears, say:

> “ELION analyzes the business’s digital customer journey and identifies operational gaps across areas like lead response, follow-up, booking, and social-to-lead flow.”

Keep this honest. ELION shows the evidence it can actually discover. It does not fabricate internal processes it cannot verify.

## 0:50–1:20 — Show the recommended automation

Say:

> “Once we identify the leak, the next step is turning that finding into an operational system.”

What to show:

- the recommended automation
- the automation pipeline or workflow visualization
- the idea that the fix is operational, not just a chat widget

If the competition allows, transition to the demo page and say:

> “Here’s what happens when a lead enters the system.”

Then run the interactive demo.

## 1:20–1:50 — Show the automation operating

Say:

> “A new lead can be captured, qualified, responded to, entered into a follow-up sequence, moved toward booking, and monitored.”

What to show:

- the demo step pipeline
- captured leads
- email / WhatsApp response experience
- booking creation
- follow-up scheduling

Important honesty note for the recording:

- the demo page is an illustrative demonstration using sample data
- no real messages are sent during the demo
- no real leads are processed during the demo

Say that plainly if the judge asks.

## 1:50–2:20 — Show reusable architecture and client isolation

Say:

> “The important part is that this isn’t built from scratch for every business. ELION uses reusable automation templates with client-specific configuration, credentials, and entitlements.”

What to show:

- the automation template concept
- client configuration / provisioning flow
- the idea that the same architecture can support multiple isolated clients

Do not create fake clients to prove this.

If asked for proof, the honest answer is:

- the architecture supports isolated client provisioning
- there is a reusable template-and-configuration model
- multi-client isolation is enforced at the API and database layer

## 2:20–2:40 — Show the live AI surface

Say:

> “I also built a production AI support layer that can answer questions about ELION while staying grounded in the platform’s actual pricing and capabilities.”

Then ask the assistant on the support page:

> “How much is the Growth package?”

Let the real response appear.

Expected answer:

> The Growth package is NGN 350,000, including a lead-to-booking system with lead qualification, automated follow-up, booking workflow, CRM integration, calendar integration, client dashboard, workflow monitoring, and post-launch support.

If the judge asks an out-of-scope question, show that the assistant declines rather than inventing an answer.

## 2:40–3:00 — Close

Say:

> “The goal with ELION is simple: find the leak, deploy the system that fixes it, and eventually measure the operational improvement.”

Then point to:

- the free audit as the front door
- the operational automation as the product
- the reusable platform as the engineering story

Do not close with fake proof.

## What to avoid saying

Do not say:

- “We’ve helped X clients”
- “Our clients recover Y leads”
- “This guaranteed Z% improvement”
- “We have case studies”
- “This is a voice agent” unless the competition specifically requires one and ELION actually has a production voice agent

The strongest story here is not “here’s my chatbot.” It is:

- a business diagnosis product
- an operational automation platform
- a reusable, configurable system
- a live production deployment
