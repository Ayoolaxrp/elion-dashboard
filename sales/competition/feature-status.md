# ELION Feature Status — Competition Entry

This page makes the competition story honest.

It separates what is actually live from what is configurable, demonstrable, or still pending client credentials.

## LIVE

These are real, operating parts of ELION in production.

- Free business audit entry
- Audit analysis using real website input
- Operational findings and leak identification
- Recommended automation output
- Pricing pages
- Contact / booking entry points
- Client portal workspace architecture
- Client provisioning and onboarding architecture
- Support Assistant with grounded knowledge
- Support Assistant logging
- Reusable automation template and configuration model
- Client isolation at the API and database layer

## CONFIGURABLE

These are real platform capabilities that become operational after client configuration.

- Lead Response System
- Follow-Up System
- Booking automation behavior
- WhatsApp response behavior
- Email response behavior
- Qualification rules
- Follow-up scheduling
- Client-specific business settings
- Per-client credentials
- Plan-based entitlements

## DEMO / ILLUSTRATIVE

These are shown to explain the experience. They are not claims of live customer results.

- /demo interactive walkthrough
- Sample leads, messages, bookings in the demo
- “Lead responded to in 8 seconds” illustrative stat
- Automation pipeline visualization in the demo

If the judge asks, say clearly:

- the demo is illustrative
- it uses sample data
- no real messages are sent during the demo
- no real leads are processed during the demo

## PENDING CLIENT CREDENTIALS

These depend on a real configured client and connected external authorization or integration.

- Live WhatsApp sending for a specific client
- Live email sending for a specific client
- Live booking flow for a specific client with connected calendar
- Live CRM sync for a specific client
- Live automation execution for a specific client

Do not present these as universally live before credentials exist.

## AI surface

The Support Assistant is live and grounded.

It can:

- answer pricing questions
- answer service questions
- explain the audit
- explain onboarding and payments
- refuse to invent customers, revenue, ROI, testimonials, or case studies
- hand off to the support form when appropriate

It should not be presented as having opinions, preferences, or guarantees it does not have.

## What to avoid in the competition

Do not present these as live proof:

- fake testimonials
- fake clients
- fake revenue
- fake ROI
- fake case studies
- invented automation statistics
- a voice agent unless ELION genuinely has one required by the competition

The strongest story is not “we built a chatbot.” It is:

- a diagnosis-first product
- a reusable automation platform
- a real production deployment
- a live, grounded AI surface
- honest separation of live vs illustrative
