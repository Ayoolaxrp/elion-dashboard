# ELION — 5-Minute Sales Demo Script

Purpose: answer ONE question for the prospect —
**"How does ELION fix the leak you just showed me?"**
This is not a feature tour. Keep it to their business.

Prep before the call (5 min):
- Open their audit in `/admin/audits` (or the audit summary you sent).
- Open `/demo` in a second tab.
- Have `sales/outreach-briefs.md` brief #N beside you.
- Screen-share ready; presenter notes only — do not read this verbatim.

---

## 0:00–1:00 — Show THEIR audit
"Before anything, let me show you what we found — this is your business,
not a generic demo."
- Their overall digital-operations score.
- The 1–2 leaks you're focused on (from their brief).
- The evidence line (what was directly observed on their site/socials).

Say: "This isn't a score for fun — it maps to where enquiries can get lost."

## 1:00–2:00 — Explain the leak
Pick ONE leak (their primary). Make it concrete:
- "Your site sends enquiries to [email/WhatsApp]. What happens after that
  first message is manual — so response speed and follow-up depend on who's
  at the desk."
- Name the business consequence as a risk: "A serious buyer who enquires on
  a weekend can wait a day for a reply — and viewings don't get scheduled
  from that first touch."
- Do NOT claim numbers the audit didn't measure.

## 2:00–4:00 — Show how ELION fixes it (the demo)
Open `/demo`, labelled illustrative:
- "This is what we deploy. Watch what happens to an enquiry."
- Walk: ENQUIRY arrives → CAPTURED → QUALIFIED → INSTANT RESPONSE →
  FOLLOW-UP scheduled → BOOKING confirmed on the calendar →
  CRM record → operator sees it on the dashboard.
- Keep narration to one line per step. Let the state transitions carry it.
- Tie each step back to their leak: "That's your WhatsApp gap closed —
  response is automatic and on-brand. That's your follow-up gap closed —
  it runs on a schedule. That's your booking gap closed — no back-and-forth."

## 4:00–5:00 — Implementation and next step
- "This is a system we configure around how you actually work — we don't
  rebuild your tools, we connect the ones you use."
- Scope for them (Growth, ₦350,000): lead response + qualification +
  WhatsApp/email + follow-up + booking + CRM + calendar + dashboard +
  monitoring. One-time fee; you own everything we build. No hidden renewal.
- Next step: "If this matches what you want, I'll send a proposal this week
  with the exact scope, timeline and what's included. Fair?"

---

## What NOT to do
- No feature tour ("and this module does…").
- No invented results ("clients typically see…").
- No pressure close on the call — the proposal is the close.
- Don't show admin internals, other prospects, or raw data.
