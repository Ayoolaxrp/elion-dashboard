# ELION — Outreach Message Templates (Batch 1 · Lagos Real Estate)

Rules that apply to every message:
- Lead with THEIR observed finding — never "I run an AI automation company".
- Only claim what the audit/Scrapling research actually observed. Never state
  lost revenue, missed leads, response-time stats, or conversion numbers the
  audit did not measure.
- Use "I noticed…", "your public journey currently shows…", "this can create a gap…".
- One finding, one question, one CTA. Replace every `[bracket]` before sending.

---

## 1. Initial WhatsApp outreach (short)

> Hi [First name], I analyzed how property enquiries move through
> [Business]'s online journey — from your site/social pages to first contact.
>
> One thing stood out: enquiries on [site] currently [specific observed
> finding, e.g. "go to an email address with no automated response", "have no
> follow-up path after the first reply"]. For a business where a serious
> buyer can be lost in that gap, that's usually worth fixing before anything
> else.
>
> I put together a short summary of what I found — worth 3 minutes?
> [link to audit finding or offer to send PDF]

## 2. Initial email (with the finding)

> Subject: What I noticed about enquiries on [Business]
>
> Hi [First name],
>

> your actual public journey.
>
> What I found:
> - [Observation 1 with evidence — e.g. "the site links to
>   Instagram/Facebook but no social page carries a working enquiry path"]
> - [Observation 2 — e.g. "contact goes to [email]; there is no visible
>   automated response or follow-up sequence"]
> - [Observation 3 — e.g. "no online booking surface; scheduling relies on

> The consequence: [business consequence phrased as risk, not proven loss —

> booking system on top of the tools you already use (WhatsApp, email,

>

> call this week? I can share the full analysis.
>
> [Name]
> ELION — elion.com.ng

## 3. No-response follow-up (value, no pressure)

> Hi [First name] — I don't want to be a nuisance. The reason I reached out
> is that the gap I saw in [Business]'s enquiry journey is the kind that
> quietly costs opportunities, and it's fixable in a matter of weeks, not
> months.
>
> I've attached the one-page summary of what I found. If it's not a
> priority right now, no problem — I'll leave it with you. If it is, I'm
> happy to spend 15 minutes showing you exactly how we'd close it.

## 4. Interested response (move to conversation)

> Great — glad it resonated. Quick context so the call is useful: the
> system we'd deploy is a lead response + follow-up + booking flow on your
> existing WhatsApp/email/calendar. No new software your team has to learn.
>
> Two questions so I can tailor the walkthrough:
> 1. Roughly how many enquiries do you get per week (site + social + WhatsApp)?
> 2. Who handles first response today, and how long does it usually take?
>
> I'll bring the findings from your audit to the call.

## 5. Demo invitation (about THEIR business)

> I'd like to show you two things on a short call:
>
> 1. Your audit — exactly where [Business]'s enquiry journey leaks (I'll
>    share the screen with your own findings, not a generic demo).
> 2. How a lead-response → follow-up → booking system would carry a real
>    enquiry through your process end to end.
>
> 20 minutes. You'll leave knowing exactly what we'd build, what it costs,
> and what you'd own. Does [day/time] work?

## 6. Proposal follow-up (professional, no pressure)

> Hi [First name] — just checking whether you had a chance to review the
> proposal I sent on [date].
>
> To recap the shape of it: [one-line scope, e.g. "lead response + follow-up
> + booking for [Business], deployed and tested, with documentation and
> post-launch support"].
>
> Happy to walk through it line by line, adjust scope, or answer any
> question about what's included vs. not. If the timing isn't right, tell me
> straight and I'll close the file respectfully.

## 7. Lost / deferred (capture the reason, stay warm)

> Thanks for letting me know. If it's useful, I'd genuinely like to
> understand what drove the decision so I can improve — was it timing,
> budget, trust, or that the problem isn't urgent enough yet?
>
> Either way: the audit findings stay valid, and I'll leave the summary
> with you. If the enquiry gap becomes a priority later, one email and we
> can pick it up from where we left off.

## Channel notes
- WhatsApp is primary for Nigerian SMBs; keep it under ~100 words.
- Email can carry the fuller evidence (2 above) with an attachment of the
  audit summary.
- Never send the same template twice to the same person; re-personalize from
  the per-prospect brief (`sales/outreach-briefs.md`) and research evidence
  (`sales/research/findings.md`).
