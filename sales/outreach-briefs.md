# Outreach Briefs — Batch 1 (Lagos Real Estate)

Each brief is built from that business's **real ELION audit** (publicly
observable signals only). Row-level status lives in
`sales/prospect-pipeline.csv`; the batch overview is in
`templates/prospect-batch-1-lagos-real-estate.md`; message templates in
`sales/message-templates.md`.

**Language rules (non-negotiable):**
- Say "I checked your public site / your public customer journey" — never
  claim internal facts you cannot see.
- "…can go quiet / creates a potential gap / is something we can automate" —
  never claim measured loss, response-time stats, or conversion numbers.
- If they challenge a finding: the audit evidence label tells you how strong
  it is (supported = directly observed; estimated = inferred). Say so.
- Deliver the full free audit in `/admin/audits` when they engage.

Tier = Growth (₦350,000) for all 20 unless the brief says otherwise — the
Growth offer (Lead Response → Follow-Up → Booking) maps to the leaks found.
"DM" = the decision maker is not yet identified; find them on the site's
contact page / WhatsApp number before sending.

### P01 — CW Real Estate (cwlagos.com) — score 34 · Tier 1
- **Observed leaks:** No WhatsApp Business auto-response; no online booking;
  website itself scores ~30/100 technically.
- **Why it matters:** cwlagos.com is a lead-generation site for luxury
  brokerage (Ikoyi/VI/Lekki). A buyer comparing agents can message and get no
  automatic reply, then have no way to book an inspection online — between
  first contact and viewing is where the enquiry can go quiet.
- **Opening (WhatsApp):**
  > Hi [Name], I analysed cwlagos.com with an operational-audit tool and
  > found something worth 60 seconds. When a potential buyer messages you,
  > there's no automatic WhatsApp reply, and there's no way to book a viewing
  > online — so an interested buyer can easily go quiet between first contact
  > and inspection. I only checked public signals, so I may be wrong on
  > detail — but if this matches what you're seeing, the full free audit is
  > yours. Want me to send it?
- **Follow-up:** "Did you get a look at the audit? The WhatsApp-response gap
  alone is usually the cheapest fix — happy to show you what it looks like."
- **Call angle:** Walk the enquiry→viewing journey on their site live.
- **Solution/Tier:** WhatsApp Lead Response + Booking + Follow-Up — **Growth**.
