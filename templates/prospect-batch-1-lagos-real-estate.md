Batch date: 4 Sep 2026 · Method: real ELION audit run against each business's
live website (production `/api/audit`, evidence-labeled findings) · All 20
audits are recorded in `/admin/audits` (lead not yet contacted — no contact

**How to use this batch:**
1. Open each audit in `/admin/audits` and read the full findings.
2. Contact the owner/decision-maker on the channel they already use (WhatsApp
   number or form on their website).
3. Lead with THEIR leak (below), not with "AI automation".
4. Offer the free audit they already received, then the Growth build (₦350k)
   when the conversation is ready.
5. When one says yes, capture the before-metrics first — use
   `templates/client-measurement-framework.md`.

> Honesty rule: these findings come from publicly observable website signals +
> industry benchmarks. They are **estimates with evidence levels**, not
> claims about internal process. In conversation, say "I checked your public
> site and it looks like X" — never assert internal facts you cannot see.

## The pattern (why this niche works)

Of the 20 audited Lagos real-estate businesses, the same three leaks appear
repeatedly:

| Leak | Appears in | ELION system |
| --- | --- | --- |
| No WhatsApp Business auto-response on enquiry | 10 of 20 (critical) | WhatsApp Lead Response |
| No online booking/appointment flow (manual call/message) | 14 of 20 | Booking Automation |
| No visible CRM / structured follow-up | 9 of 20 | Follow-Up System |

That is exactly the Growth offer: **Lead Response → Follow-Up → Booking**.
One conversation per business, not six products.
